[Apple Watch Series 12](/devices/watch/series/12/) and [Apple Watch Ultra 4](/devices/watch/ultra/4/) both ship on 18 September with the [S11](/chips/s11/), the first new Apple Watch SiP since the [S10](/chips/s10/) arrived in Series 10 two years ago.

|                    | CPU             | Neural Engine | Storage | Devices                  |
| ------------------ | --------------- | ------------- | ------- | ------------------------ |
| [S11](/chips/s11/) | 64-bit, 2 cores | 4 cores       | 64GB    | Series 12, Ultra 4       |
| [S10](/chips/s10/) | 64-bit, 2 cores | 4 cores       | 64GB    | Series 11, Ultra 3, SE 3 |

On core counts the two are identical. What the S11 adds is **Audio Intelligence** — Live Rewind, Siri Recap, Shazam and Sound Recognition running on the watch itself — which is the first time Apple has led an S-series generation with an audio capability rather than a speed or efficiency claim.

### No W-series chip on either spec sheet

[Series 11](/devices/watch/series/11/) and [Ultra 3](/devices/watch/ultra/3/) both listed the [W3](/chips/w3/) alongside their S10. Neither the Series 12 nor the Ultra 4 names a W-series chip anywhere, though both still list the second-generation Ultra Wideband chip ([U2](/chips/u2/)), Wi-Fi 4 and Bluetooth 5.3.

Apple has a long history of folding separately-branded companion silicon into the main package without announcing it, and that is the likelier reading here — the wireless block moving inside the S11 rather than a radio going away. Both watches are recorded here without a W-series chip accordingly.

Series 11 and Ultra 3 are both discontinued. The [Apple Watch SE 3](/devices/watch/se/3/) carries on with the S10, leaving it the only Apple Watch on sale still running the older SiP — and the only one still listing a [W3](/chips/w3/).
