[AirPods 5](/devices/airpods/base/5/) ship on 18 September with the [H2](/chips/h2/), the same headphone chip as the [AirPods 4](/devices/airpods/base/4/) they replace.

Apple claims up to **1.5x more Active Noise Cancellation** than the previous generation and credits a new acoustic architecture for it: a custom high-excursion driver and a high dynamic range amplifier. Neither is silicon. Bluetooth stays at 5.3, which is what the H2 has supported since it launched with AirPods Pro 2 in September 2022.

AirPods 5 does not carry the [U2](/chips/u2/) ultra-wideband chip. Among AirPods, only [AirPods Pro 3](/devices/airpods/pro/3/) does, for precision Find My tracking of the case.

Both [AirPods 4](/devices/airpods/base/4/) and [AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation](/devices/airpods/with-active-noise-cancellation/4/) are discontinued. That leaves the H2 in three products still on sale — AirPods 5, [AirPods Pro 3](/devices/airpods/pro/3/) and [AirPods Max 2](/devices/airpods/max/2/) — plus [Apple Vision Pro](/devices/vision/apple-vision-pro/). No [H1](/chips/h1/) device has been on sale since the [AirPods Max 2 replaced the last of them in March](/news/mac-pro-discontinued-airpods-max-2/).
