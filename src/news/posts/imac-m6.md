The [iMac](/devices/mac/imac/) moves to the [M6](/chips/m6/), with pre-orders from 28 October and availability from 6 November. Like the [Mac mini in September](/news/mac-mini-mac-studio-refresh/), it skips the [M5](/chips/m5/) entirely and goes straight from the [M4](/chips/m4/).

Both models step up two CPU cores and two GPU cores. The two-port model goes from the 8c/8c M4 to a new 10c/10c M6, and the four-port model from the 10c/10c M4 to the 12c/12c M6 the Mac mini already uses.

|                                     | CPU                                    | GPU      | Neural Engine | Bandwidth  | Thunderbolt |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------- | ---------- | ----------- |
| [M6 12c/12c](/chips/m6/base/12-12/) | 2 super + 4 performance + 6 efficiency | 12 cores | 2 × 16 cores  | 170.7 GB/s | 4 ports     |
| [M6 10c/10c](/chips/m6/base/10-10/) | 2 super + 3 performance + 5 efficiency | 10 cores | 2 × 16 cores  | 170.7 GB/s | 2 ports     |
| [M4 10c/10c](/chips/m4/base/10-10/) | 4 performance + 6 efficiency           | 10 cores | 16 cores      | 120 GB/s   | 4 ports     |
| [M4 8c/8c](/chips/m4/base/8-8/)     | 4 performance + 4 efficiency           | 8 cores  | 16 cores      | 120 GB/s   | 2 ports     |

### The first cut-down M6 keeps both Neural Engines

Until now the M6 came in a single configuration, in one machine. The new [10c/10c M6](/chips/m6/base/10-10/) drops one performance core, one efficiency core and two GPU cores, and keeps everything else: both super cores, the dual 16-core Neural Engine, the full media engine with ProRes RAW and AV1 decode, and the same "up to 170GB/s" memory bandwidth.

That is a different cut from the [iPad mini's A20 Pro, announced the day before](/news/ipad-mini-a20-pro/), where the smaller configuration loses half its Neural Engine. **On the iMac, both M6 configurations have all 32 Neural Engine cores.**

What the 10-core model gives up is elsewhere in the configuration. It tops out at 24GB of memory against 32GB, 1TB of storage against 2TB, and drives one external display against two. Nano-texture glass is only offered on the 12-core model.

Apple again quotes memory bandwidth as "up to" 170GB/s. On the Mac mini that figure only applies to 24GB and 32GB configurations, with 16GB limited to 153GB/s, and both iMac models start at 16GB.

Apple's comparisons are against a 10c/10c M4 iMac with 32GB of memory: up to 1.5x faster in Excel, up to 2x faster graphics and ray-traced gaming, up to 5x faster AI prompt processing in LM Studio, and up to 2x faster SSD reads and writes.

### N1 comes to iMac

The iMac now lists the [N1](/chips/n1/) for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread. It was the last M-series Mac without it, so **every current Mac except the [MacBook Neo](/devices/mac/macbook-neo/) now has the N1**. 2.5Gb Ethernet is standard on the 12-core model and an option on the 10-core.

The 10-core iMac costs $1,499 and the 12-core $1,699.

### No Mac left on M4

With the iMac moved on, no Mac uses the M4 any more. The [iPad Air](/devices/ipad/air/) and its [8c/9c M4](/chips/m4/base/8-9/) are the only M4 device still on sale.
