The [MacBook Pro 14″ PROLED XDR](/devices/mac/macbook-pro-14-proled-xdr/) and [16″ PROLED XDR](/devices/mac/macbook-pro-16-proled-xdr/) are up for pre-order today and arrive on 6 November. They don't replace the existing [MacBook Pro 14″](/devices/mac/macbook-pro-14/) and [16″](/devices/mac/macbook-pro-16/), which stay on sale, so Apple now sells two MacBook Pro lines in each size.

There is no new silicon. Both PROLED models use the [M5 Pro](/chips/m5/pro/) and [M5 Max](/chips/m5/max/) that [arrived in March](/news/march-2026-releases/), in the same three configurations as the existing 16-inch.

|                                        | CPU                      | GPU      | Bandwidth | Memory           | Storage    |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------- | --------- | ---------------- | ---------- |
| [M5 Pro 18c/20c](/chips/m5/pro/18-20/) | 6 super + 12 performance | 20 cores | 307 GB/s  | 24 / 48 / 64 GB  | 1TB – 4TB  |
| [M5 Max 18c/32c](/chips/m5/max/18-32/) | 6 super + 12 performance | 32 cores | 460 GB/s  | 36 GB            | 2TB – 16TB |
| [M5 Max 18c/40c](/chips/m5/max/18-40/) | 6 super + 12 performance | 40 cores | 614 GB/s  | 48 / 64 / 128 GB | 2TB – 16TB |

### No M6 with PROLED XDR

**The [M6 that became the 14-inch MacBook Pro's base chip](/news/macbook-pro-14-m6/) yesterday isn't offered with PROLED XDR.** The newest chip generation and the newest MacBook Pro display don't come together. Every PROLED model has a single 16-core Neural Engine, where the cheaper M6 MacBook Pro has two.

The only configuration change is storage. The M5 Max now goes up to 16TB, against 8TB in the existing MacBook Pro.

### The display engine does the work

Apple credits much of PROLED XDR's image quality to "advanced algorithms in the display engine", which adjusts every pixel for content, wear, temperature and environment at the sub-millisecond level. That display engine is part of the M5 Pro and M5 Max, so the same chips that drive the existing MacBook Pro's mini-LED Liquid Retina XDR display also drive the new tandem OLED panel. Apple first used tandem OLED in the [M4 iPad Pro](/devices/ipad/pro/).

The panel reaches 2,000 nits peak in both HDR and SDR, with a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, P3 and Adobe RGB colour, and ProMotion from 10Hz to 120Hz. It is also the first Mac with a touchscreen and with the Dynamic Island. The 16-inch display grows to 16.3 inches, and both models are lighter, at 3 pounds for the 14-inch and 4 pounds for the 16-inch.

Connectivity doesn't change: three Thunderbolt 5 ports, up to three external displays with the M5 Pro and four with the M5 Max, and the [N1](/chips/n1/) for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread. The 16-inch is rated at up to 24 hours of video streaming with the M5 Pro and 22 hours with the M5 Max, from a 100-watt-hour battery.

The 16-inch starts at $3,399 with M5 Pro and $4,799 with M5 Max.
