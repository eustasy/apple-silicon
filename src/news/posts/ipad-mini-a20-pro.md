The [iPad mini (A20 Pro)](/devices/ipad/mini/a20-pro/) is up for pre-order today and arrives on 6 November. It skips the A18 Pro and A19 Pro entirely, going straight from the [A17 Pro](/chips/a17/pro/) in the [7th gen](/devices/ipad/mini/7/) to the [A20 Pro](/chips/a20/pro/) that launched in the [iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo](/news/iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-duo/) last month. It is the first iPad on Apple's 2nm process.

There are two configurations of the chip, and which one you get depends on storage.

|                                      | CPU             | GPU     | Neural Engine | Storage       |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------- | ------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| [A20 Pro 6c/7c](/chips/a20/pro/6-7/) | 2 super + 4 eff | 7 cores | 2 × 16 cores  | 512GB         |
| [A20 Pro 5c/7c](/chips/a20/pro/5-7/) | 2 super + 3 eff | 7 cores | 16 cores      | 128GB & 256GB |
| [A17 Pro 6c/5c](/chips/a17/pro/6-5/) | 2 perf + 4 eff  | 5 cores | 16 cores      | Any 7th gen   |

### The first A-series chip binned on CPU and Neural Engine

Every earlier binned A-series chip, from the [A15 Bionic](/chips/a15/) to the [A19 Pro](/chips/a19/pro/), differed only in GPU cores. The new [5c/7c A20 Pro](/chips/a20/pro/5-7/) keeps all 7 GPU cores but drops an efficiency core and half the Neural Engine, leaving a single 16-core unit where the full chip has two.

Tying the chip to storage is how the [iPad Pro](/devices/ipad/pro/) has worked since the [M4](/chips/m4/), where only the 1TB and 2TB models get the 10-core CPU. The iPad mini draws its line lower, at 512GB. The 128GB model costs $699, the 256GB $799, and the 512GB $999, so the extra efficiency core and the second Neural Engine come with a $200 storage step.

Apple's comparisons are against the 7th gen: up to 4.5x faster AI performance, up to 2.7x faster graphics, and up to 60% faster multithreaded CPU performance, the last of which Apple attaches to the "up to 6-core CPU". It also claims over 2x the unified memory bandwidth, and says the A20 Pro sits in "a new package inspired by M-series chips". The GPU picks up the A20 generation's Neural Accelerators along with dynamic caching, mesh shading and hardware ray tracing.

### C2 and N1 come to iPad mini

The 7th gen lists neither chip. Cellular models of the new iPad mini get the [C2](/chips/c2/), making this the first iPad with Apple's second-generation modem. Apple claims up to 50% faster cellular data than the previous iPad mini, and it is eSIM only. Every model gets the [N1](/chips/n1/) for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread. As before, no Ultra Wideband chip is listed.

Outside the silicon, this is **the first iPad mini with an OLED display**. Every earlier model, back to the 1st gen, used LCD. The 8.5-inch panel fits in the same footprint as the 7th gen's 8.3-inch one, and the iPad mini also gets thinner at 5.4mm and is now rated IP67. The Wi-Fi + Cellular model starts at $849.

### Discontinued

The iPad mini (7th gen) is no longer sold. With the [iPhone 15 Pro](/devices/iphone/15/pro/) and [15 Pro Max](/devices/iphone/15/pro-max/) already gone, that leaves **no [A17 Pro](/chips/a17/pro/) device on sale**.
